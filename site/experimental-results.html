Scoring and token definitions

Paper Eq. 5 ranks all six models by each task’s best valid, within-budget score (zero if none), then averages 100 × (6 − rank) / 5 equally across tasks. Ties share average rank. The 95% intervals bootstrap tasks: 10,000 resamples for the full suite; 3,000 for filtered groups.

Visible tokens use o200k_base for agent text and tool-call content, excluding tool responses, repeated code-diff displays, and reasoning traces. Full-suite totals follow Figure 8; filtered totals sum rounded Figure 15 values (≈). First-score time is the median across scored runs in the selected tasks.
